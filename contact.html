About Me

Hello, my name is Nathan Miller

My heart has always been for the next generations. I believe that they will be the start of a revival. I received my call to youth ministries when I was about 14 years old, and doors have been opening ever since then. I feel a deep call to Reservation ministry and working with Native Americans. At some point, I also hope to be doing youth ministry overseas and spreading the gospel to those around the world.

Here is a little bit about my personal life outside of ministry. I am married, and we do not have any kids. I received my bachelor's degree in youth ministries and biblical studies at Trinity Bible College and Graduate School. I enjoy working out, being outdoors, and hanging with friends and family.
